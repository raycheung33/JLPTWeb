Akuma JLPT

Meet Akuma Sensei. He grades harder than the JLPT, because he wants you to pass it.

Import a JLPT past paper as a PDF and choose its sections. Akuma JLPT turns each one into multiple-choice questions, booklet by booklet, from N5 to N1, with furigana, a translation in your first language and an explanation for every choice.

Practise a booklet at your own pace, or sit it as a mock exam against the real time limit. When you finish, you see your score against the official pass marks, area by area, and Akuma Sensei tells you what he thinks. Pick his tone: strict, brutal, disappointed, wordy or gentle.

In review, select any word or sentence and tap Explain. The sensei breaks it down: its reading, every meaning with an example, and step by step how a conjugated form comes from its dictionary form. His explanations are kept with the question for next time.

Listening too: add the paper’s script, answers or recording, and review each question against its script.

Think the paper’s answer is wrong? Correct it, or ask the sensei to work it out. Then Retry Missed takes you through only the questions you got wrong.

The app speaks English, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Indonesian, Burmese and more, and the sensei writes his notes in your first language, or in easy Japanese (やさしい日本語).

Akuma JLPT uses Google’s Gemini with your own Google Gemini key, so you only pay Google for what you use, and your papers stay on your device.
