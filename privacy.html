Privacy Policy

Akuma JLPT · Last updated 27 November 2026

Akuma JLPT is a JLPT study app: it turns past papers you import into practice and mock exams, with notes and a strict sensei who grades your results. This page explains what the app keeps, where, and what it sends. Akuma JLPT is operated by Cheung Pak Wai, and “we” and “us” mean us.

We collect nothing about you. The app has no accounts, no analytics and no servers of ours. To read your papers and write notes, it sends them straight from your device to Google’s Gemini API, using the Google Gemini key you enter. We never see any of it.

What stays on your device

The app keeps these in its own storage on your phone or tablet:

  • the papers you import: their questions, furigana, translations, explanations and the sensei’s comments, and on iPhone and iPad a copy of the PDF, so sections can be imported later;
  • for listening, the script PDF, answer PDF and recording you add: on iPhone and iPad a copy of each, and on Android a link to the file you picked, so the recording can play during practice and review;
  • your practice and mock exam history: your answers, scores and times;
  • the explanations you asked for with Explain, kept with their questions;
  • answers you corrected with Wrong answer?, and the answers and explanations the sensei worked out for them;
  • your settings: the app’s language, your first language, the sensei’s tone, the Gemini model and Explain prompt, text size and dark mode;
  • your Google Gemini key, in the iPhone and iPad Keychain, or encrypted with the Android Keystore.

None of this is sent to us. It is removed when you delete the app, and it is included in your device’s own backups in the same way as any other app’s data, except your Gemini key, which stays on the device it was entered on: it never moves to another device through a backup or transfer.

What is sent to Google

Akuma JLPT uses Google’s Gemini API to read papers and write notes. It contacts Google only when you ask it to, with your own API key, directly from your device:

  • When you import a paper, the app uploads the PDF to Gemini to read its questions, then sends the questions’ text to add furigana, translations and explanations, and to write the sensei’s result comments. It deletes the uploaded PDF from Google when the import finishes.
  • When you import listening, the app also uploads the script PDF and answer PDF you added, and the recording only if you turn on Send audio to Gemini, so Gemini can write each question’s script from it. It deletes them from Google when the import finishes.
  • When you use Wrong answer?, asking the sensei sends the question, its choices and its passage or script, to work out the answer or rewrite the explanations for the answer you chose.
  • When you tap Explain, the app sends the text you selected and the question it is in, with only the sentence of a reading passage that holds your selection.

What Google does with these requests is governed by Google’s terms for the Gemini API and the Google Cloud account your key belongs to, not by us. Under Google’s terms, requests made with a free-tier key may be used to improve Google’s products. Requests are charged to your key according to Google’s pricing.

What you choose to share

  • Export all saves your papers, and if you choose, your history and explanations, to a file in a place you pick. It never includes your API key. Import all reads such a file back.
  • Share on the results screen makes an image of your score and the sensei’s comment and hands it to the app you pick.
  • Ratings: on Android, the app may ask once whether you enjoy it and offer Google Play’s rating dialog. A rating goes to Google Play under its terms; the app doesn’t see it.

What we don’t do

Akuma JLPT has no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no third-party trackers. It reads only the files you choose, and asks for no access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone or photos. We do not sell data, because we do not have any.

The App Store and Google Play

You download Akuma JLPT from Apple’s App Store or Google Play. Those stores handle the download and may collect information under their own privacy policies; we do not receive personal information from them about you.

Children

We collect no personal information, from children or anyone else.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app handles information differently, we will update this page and change the date at the top before that version is released.
